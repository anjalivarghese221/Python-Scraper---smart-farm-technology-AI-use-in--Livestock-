INTERPRETATION GUIDE:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
• Cohen's Kappa: Measures agreement beyond chance (0-1 scale)
  - 0.0-0.2: Slight agreement
  - 0.2-0.4: Fair agreement
  - 0.4-0.6: Moderate agreement
  - 0.6-0.8: Substantial agreement
  - 0.8-1.0: Almost perfect agreement

• Pearson/Spearman Correlation: Relationship between predictions (-1 to +1)
  - High positive: Models move together
  - Near zero: Models operate independently

• Matthews Correlation Coefficient: Balanced measure for multi-class
  - Similar interpretation to Kappa

• Confusion Matrix: Shows misclassification patterns
